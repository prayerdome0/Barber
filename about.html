Our Story

Who We Are

Born From A Simple Belief

Royal Cutz, a brand of Seedwell Investment Limited, was founded with one idea in mind: every gentleman deserves a grooming experience that feels as good as it looks. What started as a single chair and a pair of scissors has grown into a destination for men who care about their craft.

We blend the timeless traditions of barbering — hot towels, straight razors and real conversation — with modern styles, products and standards of hygiene. The result is a shop where a haircut is never just a haircut.

  • Our mission — to give every client a sharp, confident look in a space that feels premium.
  • Our standard — master-level craft, spotless hygiene and genuine hospitality, every visit.
  • Our promise — if you don't love your cut, we'll make it right. Simple as that.
